Research Background

This research examines the implicit power imbalances in cross-cultural communication, particularly the interactions between Western and Chinese cultures in a globalized context. Using overseas Chinese communities as a focal point, it investigates how cultural communication platforms (e.g., REDnote and TikTok) reveal racial privilege, linguistic power, and identity dynamics. By analyzing user behavior and interactions across diverse cultural backgrounds, the study explores how design can act as a tool to balance privilege, promote understanding, and encourage inclusivity.
